# Council Report

Review of the three Atlas mockups in `mockup/atlas/` (A Lantern Globe, B The Chart, C Two Shores). Held after the owner asked which the Council favoured; the Council had not been asked before. Plan review: `council-report-atlas-plan.md`.

## Animal Perspectives

### Bear — Vision & Direction
- A is the film. The one moment nothing else can show is the 1874 voyage with the Earth turning under it. Globe is the default.
- C is the reading view. B is neither.

### Cat — Risk & Scope
- B is the weakest: at world scale England and New Zealand are one blob each, for four centuries.
- A's hero moment happens once. Only one voyage is dated tightly enough to sail; the other crossings fade. For most of the timeline the globe shows one small cluster, and C reads better.
- Three views means polish spread three ways. Cut B.

### Owl — Process & Structure
- A and C share one engine. B costs little code but adds a button, a camera and tests. Decide the default before polishing.

### Puppy — Opportunities & Momentum
- The ship on A. The arcs across the folded ocean on C. Side by side on a wide screen is the best of both.

### Rabbit — Resources & Stakeholders
- On a phone C stacks and needs no gestures. A needs drag and pinch. Consider C as the phone default.

### Wolf — Cohesion & Balance
- C gives Poland its own inset; on the globe Poland is never visited. C leaves one person (New York) off its panels and must say so.

## Consensus

A and C together, B dropped. Globe is the default on a wide screen; side by side where there is room; Two Shores is the flat map. Open point for the owner: whether Two Shores should be the default on phones.

## Recommended Next Steps

1. Build A and C. Drop B unless the owner wants a world map.
2. Say on Two Shores who falls outside its panels.
3. Owner to choose the phone default.
