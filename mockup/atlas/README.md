# Atlas mockups

Three working sketches of the Atlas, on the real data, made to choose a direction before the build. Each is one self-contained file: open it in a browser. Press play, drag the year, hover or tap a light.

| File | Direction |
|---|---|
| `a-lantern-globe.html` | A globe that turns to follow the family, with a small flat world map in the corner |
| `b-the-chart.html` | One flat map that opens on Norfolk and pulls back as the family spreads |
| `c-two-shores.html` | Britain and New Zealand drawn large side by side, with the crossing between them |

Rebuild with `node scripts/build-mockups.ts` (source: `proto.js`, `proto.css`). Only deceased people are in the files, as in the River.
